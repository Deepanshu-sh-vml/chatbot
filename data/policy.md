# Northwind Support Policy

## Billing Policies

**[P1] Refund Window**
Refunds are available within 7 days of purchase for any reason. After 7 days, refunds are not issued. For refunds requested within the window, we process the refund within 2-3 business days to the original payment method.

**[P2] Duplicate Charges**
If a customer is charged multiple times for the same subscription or order (our error), we refund all duplicate charges within 2-3 business days, regardless of how long ago the purchase was made.

**[P3] Proration on Plan Changes**
If a customer changes their plan mid-billing cycle (e.g., from monthly to annual), we adjust the charge. Credit is applied if the new plan costs less; additional charge is applied if it costs more. Changes take effect immediately.

## Account Policies

**[P4] Password Reset**
Customers can self-serve password resets via the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. If a customer requests assistance, support sends a password reset link via email. Password reset links expire after 24 hours.

**[P5] Account Cancellation**
Customers can cancel their account at any time via the account settings page. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle (no mid-cycle refunds). After cancellation, the account remains accessible for 30 days to download data; after 30 days, the account is deleted.

**[P6] Plan Changes**
Customers can change their subscription plan at any time. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Upgrades take effect immediately with proration applied [P3].

## Technical Policies

**[P7] App Crash Troubleshooting**
Common fixes for app crashes: (1) Clear app cache, (2) Update to the latest version, (3) Restart the device, (4) Check system requirements. If crash persists, escalate to engineering; do not promise fixes.

## Shipping Policies

**[P8] Delivery Timelines**
Standard shipping: 5-7 business days. Express shipping: 2-3 business days. Expedited shipping: next business day (where available). Delivery times are estimates; delays may occur. For urgent deliveries, customers should contact support; no guarantees outside policy.

---

## Not Covered (Deliberately Uncovered)

The following topics are **NOT** covered by policy and should trigger **escalate**:
- **International shipping**: We have no policy on shipping outside the domestic region.
- **Subscription family plans**: We have no policy on discounted multi-user subscriptions.
- **Custom billing cycles**: We have no policy on non-standard billing schedules (e.g., quarterly, semi-annual).
